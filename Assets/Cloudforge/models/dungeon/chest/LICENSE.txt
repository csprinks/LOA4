Conversion notes (2026-09-16): the source .fbx files were baked to glTF, splitting
each chest into a static "base" and a "lid" under a "lid_pivot" node placed at the
rig's hinge bone. The 2048x2048 .tga maps were resized to 1024 and packed - albedo
kept its alpha for the cobwebs, roughness and metallic combined into one ORM map,
and the sparse emissive map kept as-is. The large and epic chests were duplicated
from the medium one by the original artists, who left the old gold and cobweb
meshes displaced along X rather than refitting them; those were slid back onto the
chest centreline during conversion.
